## New question and hypotheses, frozen before execution

Question ID: r10-inverse-club-candidate-completeness-v1.

The complete aligned player-record identities were already checked in R10
projection research. Repeating that result is a regression. The new question is:
does the distinct reciprocal member-list candidate subset equal the inverse
association obtained independently by scanning the *entire* captured staff table
for the known club pointer at staff offset 0x39 and a complete aligned valid
player link? This tests structural completeness of a candidate projection.

H1: the two distinct identity sets are exactly equal, with no duplicate
reciprocal complete candidate staff entries. Duplicates elsewhere in the
fifty-slot list remain separately reported. The opposite result is equally reportable: retain counts of
intersection, list-only and inverse-only identities and both set hashes.
Forty-three reciprocal complete records and two invalid/five foreign-club
entries remain separately labeled known regression observations. No guessed
player-side field or semantic squad eligibility rule enters the experiment.

## Observations and controls

Retain all fifty slots in the denominator. Record counts for invalid staff,
foreign-club entries, reciprocal staff, complete player IDs, unaligned player
links, missing player extents, wrong player IDs and duplicate staff/player
references. Record full staff-table club association counts, including invalid player-link
associations, then distinct list/inverse/intersection/list-only/inverse-only
cardinalities. Keep hashes of both identity sets, ordered complete candidate
records and the full player table. Do not emit names, addresses, raw IDs or record
bytes to public reports.

Run six defect controls. The shared player identity validator rejects an
unaligned link, changed player ID and shortened player record. Completeness
comparison detects a dropped member and duplicate member against an independently
known complete control set. The shared club-association predicate detects a
changed backpointer. Every control must discriminate even if H1 is false.
A nondiscriminating control fails execution; a contradictory hypothesis remains
a successfully completed research observation, with its boolean false and
comparison verdict not-run. Missing data or exceptions remain failed/censored,
never silently filtered.
