## Facts, comparison and bounded interpretation

Retain every instruction's address, exact bytes, size, Capstone instruction ID,
mnemonic/operand text, operand kinds and widths, explicit memory read/write flags,
base/index/scale/displacement/segment, register effects, EFLAGS metadata, branches,
direct targets, call targets and fallthrough. Keep LEA address calculation separate
from memory reads. Record implicit 32-bit stack reads/writes for POP/RET/PUSH/CALL.
These are static address expressions and instruction effects: no actual read
address/value, visited branch or trace is asserted. Call targets are dependencies,
not inspected callees. Signed relational branches and sign/zero extension are
explicitly distinguished; ordinary moves preserve bit patterns, and CMP alone
does not select signed or unsigned interpretation. Equality predicates operate
on bit patterns.

All 38 instructions are retained exactly. Seven preexisting textual anchors are
compared: the two `+0xd7` additions, count `0x32`, stride `4`, loop `jne 0090d43f`,
`cmp dword ptr [eax+0x39],ecx`, and `call 00896a90`. Full-range identity protects
the other instructions without inventing additional pre-run semantic anchors.
A complete successful inspection has execution `succeeded`; its comparison is
`equal` or `different`, independently of the hypothesis conclusion. Missing
decode/failed control is `failed` with comparison `not-run`; wrong specimen/tool
identity is `rejected`. Hypothesis conclusion is `supported` or `contradicted`
for a complete comparison and remains `unresolved` unless complete
facts are produced. No worker accepts or promotes its own findings.
