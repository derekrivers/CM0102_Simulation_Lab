# Frozen local 24-hour qualification protocol v1

This tests **LL1: approved finite local research campaigns on one PC/WSL worker**.
It does not close full P01 or P03. Execution starts only after a reviewed exact
integration revision, a successful short integrated pilot, service failure drills
and independent operator acceptance. The campaign manifest records the precise
source revision, every tool/input/source digest, measured environment and start.
The retained accepted checkout remains fixed throughout execution and replay.

## Useful work and endpoints

Use the built-in `regression` profile exactly once: actual native/WASM RNG corpus
with its retained byte identity and four defect controls; the retained original
R10 instruction corpus with its exact receipt, eleven successful/four censored
calls and twelve controls; and the qualified fixed Bosman classic same-save
profile. Bosman remains seed 1, fixed clock, exactly fourteen days and the two
`normal,again` arms. No new seeds, duration, commands or behaviour are admitted.
Execution and comparison fields remain separate; Bosman comparison stays not-run.

The new squad structural question has its separately reviewed frozen protocol
and executes as a separate pilot campaign. It never changes production admission.
Do not repeat regression work to fill twenty-four hours. After the finite queue
drains, retain explicit idle/queue accounting until the full observation horizon.

## Fixed budgets and stop conditions

One host execution slot, CPU quota 100% (one CPU), MemoryMax 2 GiB, swap zero and
128 tasks. Each attempt: elapsed limit 1,800 seconds, semantic-progress limit
660 seconds, output 2 MiB, monitored attempt disk 512 MiB, minimum free disk
1 GiB, and at most two attempts (infrastructure recovery only). The campaign has
exactly three logical jobs. Stop on the first terminal failure/rejection or any
comparison divergence. Output/disk sampling overshoot remains explicit. A
monotonic observation horizon of 86,400 seconds must actually elapse; wall-clock
jumps cannot satisfy it. A heartbeat gap over thirty seconds or a boot change
censors unfinished observation rather than crediting unavailable time.

Initial measurements were 16 logical CPUs, 11,960 MiB RAM and approximately
930 GiB free. Pre-integration approved workload execution peaked near 960 MiB
under the stated service limits; the integrated pilot must confirm the envelope.
These are measured local values, not a throughput or platform qualification.

## Failure drills, restart and accounting

The reviewed service acceptance must demonstrate active worker SIGKILL/restart,
preservation of a preceding completed result, clean old descendants, a distinct
bounded recovery attempt, cross-root exclusion, output exhaustion, stalled work,
elapsed/disk limits, cancellation, real incomplete publication and reconciliation
without rerunning the successful job. Record its exact source and receipt hashes.

During the long campaign inject one worker-main SIGKILL using a one-shot systemd
timer forty-five seconds after launch. The stable worker service must terminate
old descendants and restart before the thirty-second observation-gap boundary.
If useful work is active, retain that interrupted attempt and retry within its
existing budget. This repetition has an explicit recovery purpose. If the queue
has already drained, record idle restart instead; never claim interrupted-work
coverage from that case. The short service drill separately proves active recovery.
No model, chat reminder or agent executes campaign jobs.

Launch through the Windows entry point and verify the detached lifetime holder's
fresh acknowledged token and heartbeat, persistent foreground WSL client, and
temporary system-awake request. The operator launch shell then exits. Record
that the independent worker and holder continue. PC logoff/shutdown, explicit
sleep and WSL shutdown remain interruptions, not silently successful observation.

## Results, replay and acceptance

The generated private HTML/JSON report accounts for all three logical jobs,
every immutable physical attempt, rejected/censored/contradictory work, explicit
idle time, active time, restart gaps, resource samples and artifact inventories.
All intended successful endpoints must be reached. A correctly retained failure
alone is insufficient. Pending or corrupt publication remains visible and cannot
be reported as complete reproducible evidence.

After the actual horizon, replay at least the selected RNG result through the
supported replay command on the retained exact checkout/configuration. Preserve
the original and replay campaign manifests and attempts, verify recorded artifact
inventories and the expected canonical output identity. Independent review must
accept the exact delivered revision and relevant short/long/replay evidence.
Launching this run is **not** completing it. The report and progress record must
state actual elapsed time and any remaining acceptance work truthfully.

Remaining P01 gaps include its PostgreSQL/distributed ownership design, broader
original/backend qualification, distinct full-play and quick-result paths,
startup/load, short world-advance and targeted match scenario endpoints. P03's
twenty functions/four domains, five reviewed contracts/two domains, unsupported
claim rejection and separate actual seventy-two-hour gate remain open. Existing
squad semantics, complete lineup workflow and failed R9b provenance remain open.
